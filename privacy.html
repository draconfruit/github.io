Bitty Privacy Policy

Effective: October 1, 2026

Bitty is the support bot for the My Pet Bakery Discord server, run by the My Pet Bakery team ("we"). We are responsible for the information Bitty handles. This policy explains what Bitty stores, why, and your rights.

What Bitty collects

  • Your Discord account details: your user ID and username.
  • Your messages to Bitty: the text of direct messages you send, plus links to any files you attach.
  • Team replies: messages the team sends you through Bitty.
  • Team notes: notes the team writes about a conversation, visible only to the team.
  • Conversation records: a log of each support conversation, with timestamps.
  • Block list: if you are blocked from using Bitty, your user ID and the reason.
  • Levels (if enabled): your message count and points, linked to your user ID. Bitty does not store the content of your server messages for levels.

Bitty does not collect passwords, payment details, or anything outside Discord.

How it's used, and why we're allowed to

  • To answer your messages: passing your message to the team and sending their reply back. We do this to provide the service you asked for.
  • To keep the server safe: keeping records of conversations, reports, notes, and blocks so the team can follow up and moderate fairly. We do this based on our legitimate interest in running a safe community.
  • To run server features: like levels and ranks. We do this based on our legitimate interest in running the server.

Bitty does not make automated decisions about you.

Where it's stored

Your messages appear in a private support channel in the My Pet Bakery Discord server that only the team can see. Conversation records are stored in a database hosted by MongoDB Atlas, and Bitty runs on a server hosted by Oracle Cloud. Your information is stored and processed in the United States and may be processed in other countries where these providers operate. If you are outside the United States, this means your information is transferred there, and we rely on our providers' standard safeguards for these transfers.

Access is limited to the My Pet Bakery team.

Who can see it

Only the My Pet Bakery team, plus the providers above, who store it on our behalf. Discord also handles your messages as the platform Bitty runs on, under its own privacy policy. We never sell your information. We may share it with Discord if needed to report abuse, or if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep conversation records, notes, and blocks for as long as they're needed for support and moderation, for example to follow up on a report or handle repeat issues. When a record is no longer needed for that, we delete it. Level data is kept while you're a member of the server. You can ask us to delete your data at any time. We may keep a minimal record if it's needed for an open moderation case.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • show you the data we hold about you, or give you a copy
  • correct data that's wrong
  • delete your data
  • stop or limit how we use your data, or object to how we use it

We'll reply within one month. If you're in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, send Bitty a direct message (you'll need to be in the My Pet Bakery Discord) or email us at draccofruit@gmail.com.

Age

You must meet Discord's minimum age to use Discord and Bitty. If we learn that someone under that age contacted Bitty, we delete their data.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed, and we'll announce important changes in the My Pet Bakery server.

Contact

Send Bitty a direct message (join the My Pet Bakery Discord to reach Bitty) or email us at draccofruit@gmail.com.
